## Sources

- `rcl-year-a`: Revised Common Lectionary Year A (2025-26) readings
- `rcl-year-b`: Revised Common Lectionary Year B (2026-27) readings
- `rcl-year-c`: Revised Common Lectionary Year C (2027-28) readings
- `daily-office-year-two-2025-26`: Episcopal BCP 1979 Daily Office lectionary, Year Two (2025-26): psalms and lessons for each day
- `daily-office-year-one-2026-27`: Episcopal BCP 1979 Daily Office lectionary, Year One (2026-27): psalms and lessons for each day
- `jewish-weekly-torah-5786`: Standard Ashkenazi weekly Torah portion (parashah) lectionary with Haftarot for Jewish year 5786 (2025-2026)
- `jewish-weekly-torah-5787`: Standard Ashkenazi weekly Torah portion (parashah) lectionary with Haftarot for Jewish year 5787 (2026-2027)
- `one-year-daily-bible-reading-plan`: A canonical one-year daily Bible reading plan with Old Testament, New Testament, Psalm, and Proverb readings for each day
- `psalm-of-day-proverbs-of-day`: A simple daily lectionary with a Psalm and Proverbs chapter for each day of the year
- `presbyterian-reformed-lectionary-2026-27`: Reformed Sunday lectionary with Old Testament, Psalm, Epistle, and Gospel readings for 2026-27, including Reformation Sunday
- `coptic-lectionary-2026-27`: Simplified Coptic Orthodox Sunday lectionary with Old Testament, Psalm, Epistle, and Gospel readings for 2026-27
- `anabaptist-lectionary-2026-27`: Anabaptist / Mennonite discipleship lectionary emphasizing the Sermon on the Mount, peace, and reconciliation for 2026-27

## Source

Revised Common Lectionary scripture citations are from the Vanderbilt Divinity Library (https://lectionary.library.vanderbilt.edu).

Daily Office psalms and lessons are from the Episcopal Book of Common Prayer (1979), pp. 934-939 ("Concerning the Daily Office Lectionary"), in the JSON transcription at https://github.com/reubenlillie/daily-office.
